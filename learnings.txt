Naive Bayes(Bayes Theorem): It assumes every word as independent.
    Say there is a word xyz in a text. It sees how many times this word
    appears in positive tweets and how many times in negative tweets. Based on
    that it tells the probability if the tweet is positive or negative.

    Although it has a very naive assumption but it is used because it is
    extremely fast to train and sets a benchmark for comparision.
